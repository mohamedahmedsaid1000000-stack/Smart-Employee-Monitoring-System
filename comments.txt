
SMART EMPLOYEE MONITORING SYSTEM

Project Description:
An AI-powered camera monitoring system
for employee attendance and workplace time tracking.

MAIN FEATURES:

1. Employee Detection
   Detect people using YOLO.

2. Employee Tracking
   Track detected people across video frames.

3. Attendance
   Detect entry into the company.

4. Work Time
   Calculate time spent inside assigned desk zones.

5. Break Time
   Calculate time spent outside desk zones.

6. Finish Work
   Detect when an employee leaves through
   the designated exit zone.

7. Confidence
   Display person detection confidence.
   Identity confidence will be added
   in the face recognition module.

TECHNOLOGIES:

Python
OpenCV
YOLO
Computer Vision

IMPORTANT:
This is an initial prototype.
Tracking IDs are not permanent employee identities.
Camera zones must be calibrated.
